## False confidence patterns to reject
'Our model beats Claude' without matched benchmark. 'Free' without durable provider terms. 'Build successful' without real exit code. 'Secure' without independent evaluation. 'GitHub connected' without verified installation. 'Ready to ship' without acceptance tests.
